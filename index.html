The Events

General Information

  • All schools are requested to confirm their participation in the event(s) by filling the respective registration form provided in the event details by Saturday, 24 October 2026.
  • All the registration forms can be viewed at a glance at jigyasa.csnimage.com.
  • On-the-spot submission of the entry forms will not be entertained except for: Aesthetic Moves (Senior Art Competitions) and I Have A Dream… (Poetry Writing and Recitation and Story Writing Competition).
  • Entries for certain competitions are restricted, and the same has been mentioned in the rules for relevant competitions.
  • The participants and teacher escorts must reach Cambridge School, Noida, Senior Wing at 8:30 a.m. for registration and also collect their refreshment coupons from the Registration Counter.
  • All the participants must carry their respective school’s identity cards.
  • All participating schools are requested to share a direct contact number for information about the results of the winners on November 4 and 5, 2026.
  • The decision of the judges in every event will be final and binding. In the event of the absence of the prize winner(s) on the Prize Distribution Day, an accompanying teacher/any other representative of the participating school may collect the prize and certificates on behalf of the participants from their respective schools.
  • In case of any technical snag during a performance, a team may be asked to perform again at the discretion of the judges.
  • The host school will not compete for any prizes or the AC Deb Memorial Trophy.
  • All details for the events are enclosed.
  • Since we promote sustainability, the students are requested to bring their own water bottles and handkerchiefs.
  • For any queries, the participating schools may contact the school reception between 8:00 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. on the school phone numbers listed below.
